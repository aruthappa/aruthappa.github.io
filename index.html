ABOUT

Science for resilient landscapes.

My research focuses on developing scientifically robust and practical agroforestry solutions that contribute to climate resilience, ecosystem restoration and sustainable livelihoods.

I work at the interface of agroforestry, soil, biodiversity, carbon and nature-based solutions, with a strong emphasis on translating research into field-level impact.

“Building resilient agroecosystems requires connecting ecological science with farmers, landscapes, markets and climate solutions.”

RESEARCH

Research at the intersection of trees, climate and people.

My research explores how tree-based systems can simultaneously improve ecological resilience, resource-use efficiency, carbon outcomes and livelihood opportunities.

SCIENTIFIC VISION

From field experiments to landscape-scale solutions.

The future of agroforestry lies in integrating ecological knowledge, advanced technologies, carbon solutions and farmer-centred innovation.

My research seeks to connect these dimensions to develop evidence-based pathways for sustainable and climate-resilient landscapes.

  • Climate-resilient agroforestry
  • Carbon sequestration and biochar
  • Bamboo-based restoration systems
  • Soil health and nutrient cycling
  • Biodiversity and ecosystem services
  • Remote sensing, GIS and machine learning
SELECTED RESEARCH

Current research directions.

Cashew Agroforestry

Development and evaluation of climate-resilient cashew-based agroforestry systems for the West Coast region of India.

Bamboo & Green Economy

Research on bamboo cultivation, agroforestry, ecosystem services, value addition and market opportunities.

Carbon & Biochar

Exploring carbon-credit pathways through plantation-based biomass and biochar production and application.

Salinity Rehabilitation

Tree-centred approaches for rehabilitation of saline and degraded soils using species tolerance and ecosystem-based approaches.

Soil & Ecosystem Health

Understanding soil biological processes, nutrient cycling, biodiversity and ecosystem functioning in tree-based systems.

Geospatial & AI Applications

Application of remote sensing, GIS and machine learning for agroforestry mapping, monitoring and ecosystem assessment.
