About

Science for resilient landscapes.

I am a Senior Scientist in Agroforestry at ICAR–Central Coastal Agricultural Research Institute, Goa, working at the interface of agroforestry, natural resource management, climate resilience and sustainable agriculture.

My research focuses on developing scientifically robust and practical land-use systems that integrate trees, crops, soils and ecological processes to address climate change, land degradation, carbon management and livelihood security.

My work increasingly integrates geospatial technologies, machine learning, ecosystem assessment and nature-based solutions with field-based agroforestry research.

The overarching objective is to translate ecological science into scalable solutions for farmers, landscapes and climate-resilient agricultural systems.

Research

Research themes

My research portfolio combines agroforestry science with climate, soil, carbon, biodiversity and geospatial approaches to develop resilient and multifunctional landscapes.

01

Climate-Resilient Agroforestry

Development and evaluation of agroforestry systems for climate adaptation, livelihood security and sustainable land management.

02

Carbon & Climate Solutions

Carbon sequestration, carbon accounting, biochar-based systems and opportunities for climate-smart agricultural landscapes.

03

Soil Health & Land Restoration

Soil quality, nutrient dynamics, degraded land rehabilitation and nature-based approaches for improving ecosystem functioning.

04

Biodiversity & Ecosystem Services

Tree diversity, pollination, soil fauna, litter dynamics and ecosystem services within multifunctional agroforestry landscapes.

05

Geospatial Science & AI

Remote sensing, GIS and machine learning for agroforestry mapping, biomass estimation, forest-fire susceptibility and landscape assessment.

06

Coastal Agroforestry

Agroforestry, saline landscapes, coastal agriculture, Khazan lands and resilient production systems in the west coast region of India.
